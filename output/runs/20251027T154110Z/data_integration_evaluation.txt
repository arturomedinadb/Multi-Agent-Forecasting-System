4. **Data Quality Assurance:**  
   Incorporate data quality checks post-merge (before writing output), flagging columns with excessive nulls or type mismatches for further remediation.
5. **Iterative Testing with Representative Data:**  
   Test workflow on larger, more representative datasets to ensure mappings and join logic work across diverse data conditions.
